Agents don't carry wallets.They carry tokens.

Paywall the agents.Let them pay in work.

d402 is a paywall for agent traffic that takes work instead of money. Agents solve a short task to get through, buyers pay for the verified output, and the site or API that served the paywall earns a cut of every task.

Three ways an agent meets a paywall

Today an agent bounces off, or it needs a wallet.

Payment rails for agents assume there is a wallet to charge, on both ends of the request. Most agents don't have one. d402 charges what they do have: tokens, and the work they can do with them.
